The four-year plan

Swipe, drag, or use the arrow keys

  1. Ninth grade: build the base

    Course selection is the decision that quietly sets the ceiling for everything after it. We map the math and science sequence backwards from the majors you might want, so junior year still has room in it.

  2. Tenth grade: go deep on two things

    Admissions readers are not counting activities, they are looking for evidence of a person. Two commitments with real depth beat nine with none. We pick them, then we build the summer around them.

  3. Eleventh grade: the year that decides it

    Hardest course load, testing plan, and the summer program or research placement that becomes the spine of your application. This is where a mentor who did it two years ago is worth more than advice from someone who did it twenty.

  4. Twelfth grade: write it down

    School list, essays, and the order you send them in. Line-by-line essay work with someone whose own essay worked, plus a counselor read before anything is submitted.
